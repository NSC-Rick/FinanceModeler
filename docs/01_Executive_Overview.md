# 01 Executive Overview

## Purpose of the Application

The **Operating Model** (also known as Financial Modeler or FinLite) is a comprehensive financial planning and analysis tool designed to help entrepreneurs, business advisors, and Small Business Development Center (SBDC) consultants build transparent, deterministic financial projections for small businesses.

The application transforms complex financial modeling into an accessible, step-by-step process that empowers users to understand the financial implications of their business decisions before committing capital or making strategic changes.

## Primary Audience

### Primary Users
- **First-time entrepreneurs** planning to start a business
- **Business buyers** evaluating acquisition opportunities
- **Existing business owners** planning expansion or operational changes
- **SBDC advisors** working with clients on business planning
- **Lenders and investors** evaluating business viability

### User Characteristics
- May have limited financial modeling experience
- Need to understand "why" behind the numbers, not just "what"
- Require transparent calculations they can explain to stakeholders
- Often working with limited capital and need to optimize resource allocation
- Benefit from educational guidance embedded in the tool

## Business Goals

### For Entrepreneurs
1. **Build Confidence**: Create realistic financial projections based on transparent assumptions
2. **Secure Financing**: Generate professional financial statements for lender presentations
3. **Test Assumptions**: Model different scenarios to understand business sensitivity
4. **Avoid Surprises**: Identify cash flow gaps and capital requirements before they occur
5. **Make Informed Decisions**: Understand tradeoffs between pricing, expenses, and profitability

### For Advisors
1. **Accelerate Client Onboarding**: Standardized framework for financial planning
2. **Educate Clients**: Built-in guidance helps clients learn financial concepts
3. **Ensure Consistency**: Repeatable methodology across all client engagements
4. **Save Time**: Automated calculations eliminate spreadsheet errors
5. **Focus on Strategy**: Spend time on business advice, not spreadsheet mechanics

### For Lenders
1. **Assess Viability**: Review realistic projections with transparent assumptions
2. **Evaluate Risk**: Understand debt service coverage and cash flow adequacy
3. **Compare Scenarios**: See how different capital structures affect outcomes
4. **Verify Assumptions**: All inputs are visible and editable

## Learning Philosophy

The Operating Model is built on the principle that **financial literacy creates better business owners**. Rather than hiding complexity behind black-box calculations, the application:

### Transparency First
- Every calculation is deterministic and traceable
- No fabricated values or hidden assumptions
- All defaults are visible and editable
- Users can download and inspect all formulas

### Progressive Disclosure
- **Basic Mode**: Simple interface for straightforward businesses
- **Advanced Mode**: Unlocks working capital, capital stack, and acquisition features
- Users graduate to complexity as their understanding grows

### Contextual Education
- Inline help text explains financial concepts in plain language
- Industry benchmarks provide reference points
- Warning messages identify potential issues
- Coaching opportunities embedded throughout

### Experimentation Encouraged
- Scenario save/load enables "what-if" analysis
- Non-destructive Modeler allows temporary simulations
- Reset functionality provides safety net
- Session auto-save prevents data loss

## Core Financial Modeling Workflow

The application follows a structured workflow that mirrors how businesses actually operate:

### 1. Revenue Planning
**Business Question**: "How much money will we make?"
- Define revenue streams (products/services)
- Set pricing and volume assumptions
- Project growth rates
- Apply seasonality patterns (if applicable)
- Model startup ramp periods

### 2. Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)
**Business Question**: "What does it cost to deliver our product/service?"
- Set COGS percentage by industry
- Override COGS for specific revenue streams
- Model efficiency improvements over time
- Understand gross margin implications

### 3. Payroll & Personnel
**Business Question**: "Who do we need to hire and what will it cost?"
- Define roles with headcount
- Set compensation (salary or hourly)
- Include payroll taxes and benefits
- Project annual raises
- Classify as direct labor (COGS) or indirect (overhead)

### 4. Operating Expenses
**Business Question**: "What are our fixed costs to keep the doors open?"
- Rent, utilities, insurance
- Marketing and advertising
- Professional fees
- Office supplies and miscellaneous
- Model inflation adjustments

### 5. Financing & Working Capital
**Business Question**: "How will we fund the business and manage cash flow?"
- Configure loan terms (principal, rate, term)
- Set working capital assumptions (AR days, AP days, inventory days)
- Build capital stack for acquisitions
- Model different financing scenarios

### 6. Review & Analysis
**Business Question**: "Does this business work financially?"
- Income Statement: Profitability analysis
- Cash Flow Statement: Liquidity analysis
- Loan Amortization: Debt service tracking
- KPIs: DSCR, margins, cash balances
- Visual charts: Trends and patterns

### 7. Insights & Optimization
**Business Question**: "What are the risks and opportunities?"
- Red/Yellow/Green flags based on financial metrics
- Deal Optimizer: Find optimal capital structures
- Modeler: Test revenue/expense sensitivities
- Scenario comparison

## How the Application Differs from Traditional Financial Calculators

### Traditional Spreadsheet Approach
- **Black Box**: Formulas hidden in cells
- **Error-Prone**: Manual entry creates mistakes
- **Not Portable**: Difficult to share or version
- **No Guidance**: User must know what to do
- **Static**: Hard to test scenarios

### Operating Model Approach
- **Transparent**: All calculations visible and documented
- **Validated**: Input validation prevents errors
- **Portable**: Save/load scenarios as JSON files
- **Guided**: Inline help and industry benchmarks
- **Dynamic**: Easy scenario testing and comparison

### Key Differentiators

#### 1. Separation of Concerns
- **Engine**: Pure calculation logic (no UI dependencies)
- **UI**: User interface (no calculation logic)
- **Validation**: Input checking and error handling
- **Result**: Calculations are testable, reproducible, and auditable

#### 2. Deterministic & Reproducible
- Same inputs **always** produce same outputs
- No random values or AI-generated assumptions
- Every number can be traced to a user input
- Perfect for lender presentations and due diligence

#### 3. Educational Design
- Built-in coaching moments
- Industry benchmarks for context
- Plain-English explanations
- Progressive complexity

#### 4. Time Mode Flexibility
- **Monthly Mode**: 36-month projections (3 years × 12 months)
- **Annual Mode**: 3-year projections
- Automatic conversion of growth rates and calculations
- Consistent results regardless of mode

#### 5. Working Capital Intelligence
- Accounts Receivable (AR) tracking
- Accounts Payable (AP) tracking
- Inventory management
- Cash flow impact of working capital changes
- Startup vs. Acquisition mode handling

#### 6. Capital Stack Planning
- Model complex acquisition financing
- Balance uses and sources of funds
- Test different equity/debt mixes
- Optimize for DSCR and cash flow

#### 7. Scenario Management
- Save complete model state as JSON
- Load previous scenarios instantly
- Compare multiple scenarios
- Export to Excel for external analysis

## Success Metrics

A successful use of the Operating Model results in:

1. **Entrepreneur Confidence**: User understands their financial projections and can explain them to others
2. **Realistic Projections**: Assumptions are grounded in industry benchmarks and market research
3. **Adequate Capitalization**: Cash requirements identified before business launch
4. **Viable Debt Service**: DSCR above 1.25 indicates sustainable debt load
5. **Positive Cash Flow**: Business generates cash after debt service and owner compensation
6. **Informed Decisions**: User can articulate tradeoffs and sensitivities

## Application Philosophy: Transparency Over Simplicity

The Operating Model prioritizes **transparency and education** over simplicity. While this creates a steeper initial learning curve, it produces:

- **Better Business Owners**: Users learn financial concepts, not just fill in forms
- **Stronger Loan Applications**: Lenders trust transparent, well-documented projections
- **Fewer Failures**: Entrepreneurs identify problems before spending money
- **Sustainable Businesses**: Realistic planning leads to adequate capitalization

The application assumes users are intelligent and capable of learning. Rather than dumbing down finance, it makes finance accessible through clear explanations and contextual guidance.
